# 0.2.0 — salvage tugs and hires (2026-11-21)

- **Hub breaks.** A destroyed pirate hub leaves its payout in a strongbox, and its wrecks leak faint pings you can
  track. The gang later re-forms at another star.
- **Salvage tugs.** Shipyards keep a small tug fleet that clears broken hubs and unclaimed wrecks, offline and live.
  A live tug cuts the hull with a mining beam, slab by slab. Yields come from the scenario's own recipes and prices.
- **Hire a tug.** Hire one at a shipyard's desk or call one in. It follows you (through warp too) and cuts the
  wrecks you point at. When you leave, it carries on in the simulation. Dismiss it to sell the hold at the yard and
  get your share of the sale.
- **Shipyards rebuild lost traders.** Replacements take real time to build, and there are rumours about what's on
  the slip.
- **New hail menu.** Pages are made of clickable lines, and Talk is sorted by topic. Word of mouth means nearby
  crews retell what other traders remember about you, and that fades over time.
- **Better flight.** Ships steer around stations and other ships, back off when stuck, and warp out if they're
  boxed in.
- **One lifecycle for everything that goes live.** Traders, pirates, tugs and wrecks all hand over between the
  simulation and the game the same way. That means fewer ships vanishing or showing up twice, including across
  playfield switches.
- **Loot.** What doesn't fit in a wreck's crates spills into loot sacks. Cleared wrecks turn back into beacons. Live
  pirates rob what they kill, and dead ships drop their pocket cash.
- **Trade.** There's now one trade window that settles on Done. The old TRADE_PAD kiosk is gone.
- **Custom tug hull.** A server owner can drop in their own `LS_Tug` prefab.

Most of this has been tested on a test server, but not all of it. Expect rough edges.
