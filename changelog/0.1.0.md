# 0.1.0 — first public snapshot (2026-11-17)

The first public source drop. The mod info file still said 0.0.1 at the time.

- **The galaxy keeps going without you.** An offline simulation runs traders, pirates and patrols between the save's
  real stations and systems. When you're close, its ships become real ships, and they go back into the simulation
  when you leave.
- **Ships fly on their own.** The mod has its own flight controller for berthing, warping out, following, orbiting,
  fleeing and patrolling. It learns each hull's real thrust as it flies.
- **Hailing.** Use the `LS_HAIL` cockpit switch or the COMMS LCD to pick a contact, then Talk, Trade or ask for Jobs.
  Docked ships tell you when they undock, and stations chatter nearby.
- **Trade with ships.** You can buy from a trader's hold and sell into it through the game's item-exchange window.
  Each trader has a purse per trip, and some crews ask for things they want.
- **Escorts.** Traders sometimes ask for an escort to their warp point. A damaged ship pays more. Pirates come
  along for the trip.
- **Pirates.** They set ambushes and run hubs with listening posts, packs and ranks. You can pay one off. They have
  bounties and hold grudges, and they flee and taunt when you hurt them.
- **Patrols.** Station patrols answer pirate attacks in protected systems.
- **Wrecks and loot.** Wrecks get beacons and carry their real cargo. Pirates haul wrecks back to their hubs.
- **Rumours, maydays and LCD pages.** These are built from what actually happened in the simulation, and the LCD
  pages include an ASCII radar.
- **Repairs.** Damaged traders limp to a yard and get fixed there.
- **Fresh saves.** The mod also starts on a new save made from a scenario's templates.
